# Yaba demo data

I froze a small openstreetmap extract around yaba, lagos on 2026-10-04; i keep the exact geojson and source notes beside this document.

I fetched the map api bbox `3.370,6.510,3.380,6.520`, kept street ways with their source way ids, node ids, direction tags, and geometry, and omitted street names. i use the imported coordinates only to build a local graph; i do not claim complete or current street coverage.

I publish this derived street data under odbl 1.0; i credit openstreetmap contributors beside every rendered map and link to the [copyright page](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) and [license text](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).

I use every selected closure as a synthetic scenario; i do not represent a live road closure or a field observation.
